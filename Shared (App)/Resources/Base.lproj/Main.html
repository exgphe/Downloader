SAFARI EXTENSION

Keep the moments.

Save Instagram photos and videos, and X videos, straight to Photos.

Instagram ↗X ↗→ Photos

A little setup. Then one tap.

1

Enable in Safari

Turn on Downloader and allow access to Instagram and X.

2

Allow saving to Photos

Downloader only needs permission to add photos and videos.

3

Connect your video service

Open Downloader in Safari’s toolbar and enter your yt-dlp service address and access token. Images can be saved without this service.

↓

On a post, tap the new download button beside Send on Instagram or Share on X. All media in the post is saved together.
